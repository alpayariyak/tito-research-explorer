What the measurements establish

One model, one recipe and one tokenizer snapshot. The nine source groups include training, evaluation and task-selection dumps.

The corpus counts aligned recorded model turns in included trial dumps. Failed or unmatched dumps are reported separately. Observed event examples are capped; their type and region distributions are not complete corpus incidence rates. Packing multipliers are proxies from recorded histories, not measured end-to-end training cost.

Design convergence is not service certification. The new GPU training pilot and the paper's reward comparison have not established a result. Interactive token IDs, likelihoods and simulator scenarios are explicitly illustrative.

Design & evidence notes

TM-SEG keeps an immutable record of each prompt, sampled action and returned likelihood. It builds the next prompt from the declared transcript compiler. Compatible calls share a training sequence; a changed prefix starts a new sample with the actual next prompt. Repeated history supplies context, while each eligible sampled action token receives one learning label.

Independent segments use ordinary training samples. Sharing a canonical trunk with separate action branches is a proposed engine extension, with extra restrictions for models that also carry recurrent and convolutional state. Structural checks and numerical service checks are separate; neither establishes a reward benefit on its own.

The proposed study

The experiment separates the memory used for training from the memory used during evaluation. It aims to measure deployment reward effects and the cost of exact training. Proposed budgets, power calculations and coupling assumptions remain scenarios. One trained adapter per condition limits general claims about training methods.

The intended pilot

The pilot checks captured action evidence, training prefixes, numerical agreement, declared-compiler fidelity, cost and execution records. Missing likelihoods remain missing; incomplete trajectories are excluded explicitly. A terminal action does not require an unused next compilation. Matched compiler behavior is distinct from verified behavior of a deployed message frontend.
